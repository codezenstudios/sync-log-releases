# Sync Log — downloads

Sync Log keeps your terminal sessions and coding agents running on your computer, and lets you reach them from your phone, tablet or any browser.

This repository only hosts the installers. Get them from **[Releases](https://github.com/codezenstudios/sync-log-releases/releases/latest)**, or from the project page: **https://aitoolsforcoding.com/projects/sync-log/**

| Platform | File |
| --- | --- |
| Android 8.0+ | `SyncLog-<version>.apk` |
| Arch Linux / Omarchy | `synclog-<version>-1-x86_64.pkg.tar.zst` — `sudo pacman -U synclog-*.pkg.tar.zst` |
| macOS (Apple Silicon / Intel) | `Sync-Log-<version>-mac-arm64.zip` / `-mac-x64.zip` |
| iPhone and iPad | No download: pair from Safari and add Sync Log to your Home Screen |

## Connect your phone
1. Install Sync Log on your computer and open it. It shows a QR code.
2. Choose **Same Wi-Fi** or **Anywhere**.
3. On Android, open the Sync Log app and tap **Scan the QR code**. On iPhone or iPad, choose *iPhone, iPad or browser*, scan with the Camera, and follow the steps to add it to your Home Screen.
